Phase 0a frames for Spaces phases 1 and 2, in the Circaso design system (tokens and components copied from the canvas). Every flow has its empty, loading, offline and error states. Plan: references/spaces-plan.md.

The new tab between Chats and Wallet: Chats | Spaces | Wallet | You. Moments on top, spaces below. Phase 1 ships the Moments row with an honest card where spaces will go; the phase 3 version is shown for direction.

Camera, text, gallery and voice. Camera and composer screens sit on the dark stage in every theme, as the app's photo viewer does; their controls are glass over the picture.

GW · Groups with wallets, fees and limits

42 screens · Phase 2 and 2F

Chamas and groups become one Group. A wallet is optional (a switch at creation, or later from group info) and a target needs one. Every approval names the wallet. The Pay screen gains Paying from and one fee line (gas included); group daily caps show before signing.

Design decisions in the gaps

  • Money leaves a group wallet only through an approved request (option A in the plan). When the owner switches Paying from to a group wallet, Send becomes Request Payout; the PIN signs only from an approved card (Pay Payout, amount locked). Space wallets have no approval rule and keep Send.
  • Pay is the C4 sheet ("Send", "Pay Payout", "Request Payout", "Contribute"), over the chat it was opened from. Once quoted, the fee and the rate are held for the challenge's 15 minutes, so the rate line counts that down instead of C4's 30-second refresh.
  • The daily cap counts what leaves the wallet, fee included, over a rolling 24 hours. The server's resetsAt means "when this amount fits", not when the whole cap clears; the copy says "available from 14:30" accordingly.
  • Creating a group, setting up a group wallet and adding a target are online-only (the server creates the group and needs the members' keys; Circle needs a live challenge). They are disabled offline with "Needs a connection", never failed after the tap. Recorded in offline-first.md.
  • A group is kept when its wallet set-up fails or the connection drops mid-way. The wallet waits for the person (Finish Wallet Setup) and is never made silently.
  • D1's "Who can spend from the wallet" stays as "Who approves payouts: 2 admins / Any admin" (2 admins by default; every admin if fewer, as today).
  • The wallet chip shows only for people who own a group or space wallet. The personal wallet is named on approval only then; otherwise it keeps "Enter your PIN to send".
  • The fee row shows the rate, or "minimum" when the gas floor applies; never a separate gas line. The button carries the total the person signs.
  • Moment rings are monochrome: unseen is an ink ring, seen a quiet one; the accessible label says which. Ring 68, item 72: proposed as CircasoSizes.momentRing / momentItem.
  • Text moment backgrounds reuse existing token values: Ink (ink), Metal (money-card metal), Paper (fill) and Graphite (ink-secondary) from the light theme, fixed rather than themed so a moment looks the same to everyone. Text is Sans Bold or Regular; the rounded face stays for money.
  • Camera, composer and viewer are always on the dark stage in every theme, like the app's photo viewer; controls are glass with fixed white text. In high contrast the glass turns solid black at 0.8 and secondary white text goes to full white.
  • LIVE is monochrome (action fill with a soft pulse), not the error red, so red keeps meaning "something is wrong".
  • Posting a moment is a queueable write (not money): optimistic, Waiting offline, posts on reconnect; its 24 hours start when it posts.
  • The Spaces tab icon is a ring with a centre and two small circles: people around a place, drawn from the Circaso mark.
