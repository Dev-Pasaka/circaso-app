## A. Asha is self-contained

Asha is close to a product of her own (she'll have Asha credits, bought through Play Store / App Store billing, kept
separate from the money features). So everything about Asha lives inside Asha's chat:

- A settings button in Asha's chat header opens Asha's settings in place (a panel or a pushed page inside Asha, not the
  You tab): **voice** (the picker with samples that is today `AshaVoiceRoute`, reached from You), and later **credits**
  (balance, buy more) and anything else Asha-only.
- Remove the Asha entries from the You tab once they live in Asha. Nothing about Asha should need leaving Asha.
- Billing for credits is a later phase; leave the seam (a "Credits" row that's hidden until it exists, no fake states).

## B. The mascot: Asha you can see working

### Look

- A **simple 2D mascot** in the Duolingo spirit (not 3D, not realistic): built from Asha's silver mark (the disc with
  the ring and sparks) into a rounded character with expressive eyes, a mouth and small hands.
- **Two looks**, one for the female voices and one for the male voices; the chosen voice picks the look.
- Tool: **Rive** (Pasaka has the Cadet plan for **one month only**: build every state and export every `.riv` within
  that month; exported files keep working). Built by Claude through Rive's official MCP (Rive Early Access desktop app,
  `claude mcp add --transport http rive http://127.0.0.1:9791/mcp`; keep the app open). Community files under CC BY
  may be remixed for structure, with credit in the app's About page. Use **data binding** properties rather than state
  machine inputs (Rive deprecated inputs).
- Runtime libraries approved: `app.rive:rive-android` and `RiveRuntime` (iOS), latest stable at build time, wrapped
  behind our own `AshaAvatar` composable (expect/actual or a Swift bridge, like the camera).
- Fallback if Rive falls through: Lottie JSON generated by a script in the repo + Compottie (KMP, MIT); ask first.

### Three independent layers

They combine freely, e.g. beach + excited + searching on her phone for flights.

1. **Activity**: what she is doing right now.
   - idle (breathing, blinking)
   - listening (leans in)
   - thinking (eyes up, pondering)
   - searching_web (pulls out a phone and scrolls)
   - reading_transactions (types at a small desk)
   - making_chart (draws on a mini board)
   - writing_statement (writes on paper)
   - speaking (mouth driven by her voice's loudness)
   - done (nod or small wave)
2. **Mood**: her expression.
   - calm
   - happy
   - excited
   - concerned (overspending, a failed payment)
   - proud (a goal reached)
   - thinking
3. **Scene**: the environment, from what the conversation is about.
   - office (default)
   - vacation (beach, sun, palms, suitcase)
   - workout (gym: flexing, push-ups)
   - running (track or park, jogging)
   - saving (piggy bank, vault)
   - investing (stock screens, rising and falling charts)
   - crypto (coins orbiting: USDC, ETH, SOL)
   - bills / home
   - school fees
   - shopping
   - celebration
   - support (a help desk, for part C)

### How it is driven

- Asha's backend tags each turn with `{ scene, mood, activity }`, only from the fixed lists above, alongside her
  existing live status events (today `AshaEvent.Status(label)` / `LiveVoiceEvent.Status(label)` carry free text; add
  the three kinds as new optional fields, additive). No extra model call: it comes with the answer.
- **The scene changes only on a clear, sustained topic change**, crossfaded, never flickering message to message.
- **Real numbers stay real**: any price or chart in a scene comes from Circaso's own data (or is generic), never from
  the model's text.
- The real content (transactions, charts, statements) still appears in the chat as now; the mascot shows what she is
  doing, not the data.
- Placement: a small mascot in Asha's chat header, larger in voice mode. Reduced motion: a still pose per state, scene
  fades only. Offline: idle in the office. Must be light on battery (Rive renders on the GPU; pause when off screen).

### Build order within the Rive month

1. The character (both looks), every activity and mood, scenes office, vacation, workout, crypto.
2. Scenes running, saving, investing, bills.
3. The rest (school fees, shopping, celebration, support).

Each scene is its own layer or artboard so scenes can be added later without touching the character. Save the `.riv`
sources and exported stills under `design/asha/` in the repo.

**Show Pasaka stills of every state and a few combinations for approval before wiring anything into the app.**

## C. Support through Asha

Support moves into Asha: she is the first line, because she already knows what the person was trying to do.

- **Troubleshoot first.** When someone describes a problem, Asha investigates with what she can see:
  - their recent transfers and their states;
  - failed payments and their error codes and request ids;
  - account and device state;
  - app version;
  - the offline outbox (items waiting or failed);
  - known issues.

  She explains in plain words and walks them through fixes ("Send it again", "turn on fingerprint", "update the app").
- **If she can't fix it**, she asks: "Shall I open a support ticket for this?" Only on a yes does she create it, on the
  person's behalf.
- **The ticket she writes is better than one a person would**: a short summary, what the person tried, what Asha
  found (ids, error codes, request ids, timestamps, app version, device), and the steps already taken. She shows the
  person the ticket summary and its reference before sending, and confirms after.
- **Privacy**: no PINs, recovery words, keys or full card or phone numbers in tickets; the person sees exactly what is
  sent; amounts only when the issue is about that payment.
- The mascot's **support** scene (a help desk) and a "concerned → happy" mood arc fit here.
- Ticket follow-up (status, replies, how support answers, notifications) is **open: decide later** (Pasaka,
  2026-11-04). Today's related pieces:
  - support accounts and the admin site are on hold (D-S1–D-S3 in `project-status.md`);
  - lost-account recovery is a support case through `POST /v1/recovery/requests` (I15–I17).
- **Backend: MISSING.** Asha needs a tool to read the diagnostics above and a `create_support_ticket` tool backed by a
  new support-ticket endpoint (proposed: `POST /v1/support/tickets` with summary, category, details, related ids;
  idempotency key; returns a reference). Per skill rule 1, don't invent the endpoint in the app: design it in the
  backend first, then build the app behind a flag.
