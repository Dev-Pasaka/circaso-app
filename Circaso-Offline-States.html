Offline: waiting to send, and back online.

The two missing states, designed to sit beside G1 Offline and G2 Send failed. These are also on the System states page of the canvas.

Behaviour

  • Messages typed offline are kept on the phone and send automatically, in order, as soon as the connection is back.
  • Money never queues. Wallets are user-controlled, so a payment can only be signed and sent while the phone is online. Offline, the money chip still shows, but the send button is dimmed and the strip says "You're offline. Money sends only while you're connected."
  • Back online: once waiting messages go out, a toast says how many were sent ("Back online · 2 messages sent"). It shows once, for about 3 seconds, and only if something was waiting. The money the person started can then be sent normally.
  • If a send fails after reconnecting, the G2 Send failed state applies.
