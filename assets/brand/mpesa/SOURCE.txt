M-PESA logo: where these files come from
=========================================

Source (downloaded 2026-11-23):
  Safaricom PLC, Brand Toolkit, Logos page: https://www.safaricom.co.ke/about/brand-toolkit/logos
  - "M-PESA Logo", vector PDF: https://www.safaricom.co.ke/images/lifeimages/M-PESA-logo-pdf.pdf
    Kept here unchanged as M-PESA-logo-safaricom.pdf (440,185 bytes; Adobe Illustrator CC 22, PDF/X-4,
    created 2020-09-23, modified 2021-07-29; title "M-PESA Logo").
  - The reversed logo (white on green), PNG: https://www.safaricom.co.ke/images/lifeimages/M-PESA-logo-2.png
    (12500 x 6537; not kept here: only its colours are used, see below).

Made from it by scripts/make_mpesa_logo.py (rerun it if Safaricom's file changes):
  - mpesa-logo.svg, and the app's Compose resource shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/mpesa_logo.xml:
    the PDF's five shapes with every path and curve as drawn, only moved so the logo starts at 0,0 (not scaled,
    stretched or cropped). Colours: the PDF's CMYK turned into sRGB through the PDF's own output intent
    (U.S. Web Coated (SWOP) v2, relative colorimetric): letters #3CB554, phone #AEB7AA, flag #E24F50, flag shade #9E564F.
    For white or light ground only.
  - mpesa-logo-reversed.svg and mpesa_logo_reversed.xml: the same shapes in the colours of Safaricom's reversed logo
    (M-PESA-logo-2.png): letters and phone #FFFFFF, flag #CE383B, flag shade #834E44. For green ground (M-Pesa's card
    and chip in the app).

Not used: Wikimedia Commons "File:M-PESA LOGO-01.svg" (public domain as a text logo, trademarked) is a community
trace of an older uppercase logo with its source marked unknown, so it is not Safaricom's file.
